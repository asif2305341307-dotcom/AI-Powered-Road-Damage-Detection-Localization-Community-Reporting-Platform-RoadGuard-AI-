RoadGuard AI

Intelligent Road Damage Detection, Localization, Severity Assessment & Community Reporting Platform
DetectLocateReportMonitor

Our Mission

Make road damage reporting more organized, accessible and data-driven, with human verification at the center.

RoadGuard AI supports reporting and review. It does not replace road engineers or make official repair decisions.

The Problem

Potholes, cracks and surface wear are reported through disconnected channels: incomplete details, inconsistent categories, duplicate reports and no visibility into progress.

Our Answer

One workflow joining image classification, location, report management and administrator verification.

Core Features

Community ReportingSubmit a road image with GPS or a map-selected location, add a description, get a unique report ID and track status.
AI ClassificationA trained computer-vision model predicts a damage class with a confidence score, shown as an estimate for human review.
Damage LocalizationA bounding box marks the visible damage when a suitable detection model and annotations are available.
Map & Report ManagementList and map views, stored predictions and timestamps, admin verify, reject, reclassify and status history.
Duplicate DetectionFlags candidates using distance and image similarity for admin review. Nothing is auto-rejected.
Analytics DashboardReport counts by status and damage category, with filters and map visualization where feasible.

How It Works

01User submits road image and location
02App validates image and required info
03CNN predicts damage class and confidence
04Report created with unique ID, Pending Review
05Stored and shown on list and map
06Duplicate check flags similar reports
07Admin verifies, corrects, updates status
08User tracks report status

AI Approach

Dataset: public road-damage data, licence and labels verified
Split: train, validation, test kept separate to avoid leakage
Model: transfer learning with a pretrained CNN
Localization: YOLO if annotations and resources allow

Responsible AI

AI predictions stay separate from admin-verified results
Location is permission-based or manual
Bias checked across road types, lighting and weather
Outputs are never proof of road safety or repair priority
